A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT WONDER

Good questions.

How do I get Nexus Notes?

Create a website account, confirm your email, then sign in to the downloads page. Choose the Windows installer or the Mac disk image. Your website account is separate from your desktop notes.

Does local AI need AI credits?

No cloud AI credits are needed for local Ollama models. They use your computer’s memory and processing power. Model downloads require internet access and can be large; performance depends on your hardware.

Can I use it without an internet connection?

You can use local desktop notes without a connection. Downloading the app, confirming your website account, checking for updates, and initially setting up AI require internet access. Local AI needs an installed model and access to Ollama on your computer.

Which computers does it support?

Windows and macOS. The downloads page identifies the available installer and Mac architecture. The current Mac release is for Apple silicon, including M1, and requires macOS 12 or later.

Are the installer files private?

This website asks you to sign in before showing download links. The installers are published in the public Nexus Releases repository on GitHub and can also be accessed there.
